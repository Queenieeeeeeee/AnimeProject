📊 Anime Database Analysis - Dashboard Summary
🎯 Project Overview
Comprehensive analysis of 16,105+ anime titles from MyAnimeList, featuring 4 interactive dashboards that provide business intelligence insights into the anime industry.
Data Source: MyAnimeList (via Jikan API)
Tools: Tableau Public, Python (data collection), SQLite
Period Covered: 2004-2026

Provide a high-level snapshot of the anime database, showing key metrics and fundamental distributions.

🎬 Dashboard 3: Genre & Studio Analysis
Purpose:
Deep dive into content creators and genre performance, identifying industry leaders and quality patterns.
Key Visualizations:

Top 20 Studios by Average Score (Bar Chart)
Top 10 Studios by Production Count (Bar Chart)
Genre vs Score Distribution (Box Plot with Outliers)
Top 3 Studio Production Trends (Line Chart)

Key Insights:
✅ Studio Quality:

Kyoto Animation achieves highest avg score (7.51) with 114 titles
Motion Magic (score 7.68, 18 titles) and Shuka (score 7.64, 23 titles) excel with smaller catalogs
Quality ≠ Quantity (high-output studios don't always score highest)

✅ Studio Productivity:

Toei Animation leads with 343 titles (most prolific)
Sunrise (308), J.C.Staff (289) are also highly productive
Top 10 studios account for ~15% of all anime production

✅ Genre Quality Patterns (Box Plot Analysis):

Mystery and Drama genres show highest median scores
Comedy has the widest score variance (hit-or-miss)
Sci-Fi has many outliers (low scores)

✅ Production Trends:

Major studios show fluctuating production volumes
No clear long-term growth/decline for individual studios
Industry is competitive with no single dominant player

Business Implications:

Quality strategy: Partner with boutique studios like Kyoto Animation
Volume strategy: Leverage prolific studios like Toei Animation
Genre choice significantly impacts score potential
Mystery/Drama genres offer higher quality floor


👥 Dashboard 4: User Engagement & Popularity Analysis
Purpose:
Explore relationships between audience engagement metrics (members, favorites) and quality (score), uncovering hidden gems and popularity patterns.
Key Visualizations:

Member Count vs Score (Scatter Plot with Trend Line)
Favorites vs Score (Scatter Plot with Trend Line)
Top 20 Most Popular Anime (Bar Chart)
Hidden Gems (Table: Score ≥8.0, Popularity >5000)

Key Insights:
✅ Popularity-Quality Relationship:

Strong positive correlation between Members/Favorites and Score
Higher member count generally indicates higher quality
Trend line shows clear upward trajectory
Few "overhyped" anime (high popularity, low score)

✅ Most Popular Titles: (based on members)

AOT is the most popular one
Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood leads with 3,603K members
Death Note, One Punch Man, Sword Art Online all exceed 3M members
Popular anime tend to be mainstream shonen or highly accessible

✅ Hidden Gems Discovered:

20+ titles with score ≥8.0 but low popularity (rank >5,000)
Examples: Doupo Cangqiong: San Nian Zhi Yue (8.55), Chiikawa (8.47)
Many are Chinese donghua or niche Japanese productions
Excellent quality but limited marketing/awareness

✅ Fan Engagement:

Favorites vs Score shows even stronger correlation than Members
Users who "favorite" anime are more discerning
High-quality anime retain dedicated fan bases

Business Implications:

Marketing matters: Great content needs visibility to succeed
Hidden gems represent untapped IPs for licensing/promotion
Quality is discoverable: Good anime eventually find their audience
Niche markets: Chinese anime (donghua) shows high quality potential
